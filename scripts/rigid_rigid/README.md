# Rigid–rigid scenes

Scenes whose primary interaction is between rigid bodies. There are 40;
`../scene_registry.json` is authoritative and each scene's README gives its physics, cases,
requirements and the commands that rebuild it.

- [`ball_block_building`](ball_block_building/README.md): A wooden ball rolls into a two-block pile on a table, pushing the base block aside so the upper block falls.
- [`ball_box_rebound`](ball_box_rebound/README.md): In a playroom, a star-patterned toy ball rebounds from a white toy chest and strikes a football.
- [`basketball_corner_rebound`](basketball_corner_rebound/README.md): A basketball rolls into a room corner, rebounds off both walls and rolls back out.
- [`basketball_court_bounce`](basketball_court_bounce/README.md): A 24 cm basketball released 1.5 m above an indoor maple court makes diminishing bounces.
- [`basketball_cup_collision`](basketball_cup_collision/README.md): A thrown basketball knocks over an insulated metal bottle on a desk, which slides across the desk to the wall.
- [`basketball_rim_roll_in`](basketball_rim_roll_in/README.md): A basketball banks off the backboard into the rim, circles inside it and drops through.
- [`bathroom_stool_rebound`](bathroom_stool_rebound/README.md): A wooden stool thrown at a bathroom washbasin cabinet glances off and rebounds towards the bathtub.
- [`bathroom_tennis_throw`](bathroom_tennis_throw/README.md): A tennis ball hurled across a bathroom hits the shower glass, a wall, the floor and the vanity in turn.
- [`bowling`](bowling/README.md): A 5 kg ball rolls down an indoor lane into three pins standing in a triangle.
- [`chalk_box_stack`](chalk_box_stack/README.md): A board eraser swipes the bottom carton out from under two stacked chalk boxes and the upper box drops one level.
- [`curling_collision`](curling_collision/README.md): Three granite curling stones on indoor ice: red strikes blue, which slides on and strikes yellow.
- [`domino_chain`](domino_chain/README.md): Five ivory dominoes on a green-baize card table fall in sequence by contact alone, under a quarter of standard gravity.
- [`door_basketball_collision`](door_basketball_collision/README.md): A hinged door swings into a room, strikes a resting basketball and sends it rolling.
- [`flatbed_speed_bump`](flatbed_speed_bump/README.md): A flatbed truck crosses a speed bump; its two cartons are thrown and one slides off the tail onto the road.
- [`home_basketball_door`](home_basketball_door/README.md): A basketball thrown across a bedroom knocks a half-open door almost shut, rebounds and rolls to a stop.
- [`kitchen_bowl_fall`](kitchen_bowl_fall/README.md): A bowl slides out of an open wall cupboard, falls onto a bowl on the hob and shoves it aside.
- [`kitchen_can_collision`](kitchen_can_collision/README.md): A rolling can hits an upright can on a kitchen table and pushes it forward.
- [`kitchen_orange_collision`](kitchen_orange_collision/README.md): A whole orange rolls across a breakfast table into an orange with its top sliced off and knocks it rolling.
- [`kitchen_tableware_collision`](kitchen_tableware_collision/README.md): A celadon dish slides into an ivory dish on a kitchen island; both settle.
- [`kitchen_tin_collision`](kitchen_tin_collision/README.md): On a bistro table a sliding tuna tin strikes a sardine tin off-centre and sends it sliding away with a twist.
- [`newtons_cradle`](newtons_cradle/README.md): The end ball of a five-ball desktop Newton's cradle is released; the impacts pass along the row and decay.
- [`office_desk_kettle_tip`](office_desk_kettle_tip/README.md): A kettle on an office desk topples, knocks over a pen cup and flings out the pen inside.
- [`opposing_pendulum_collision`](opposing_pendulum_collision/README.md): Two balls on cords, released from mirrored 35° angles, collide head-on near the bottom and swing back.
- [`pendulum_box_collision`](pendulum_box_collision/README.md): A wooden block slides along a lab bench into a swinging pendulum bob.
- [`pool_break`](pool_break/README.md): The cue ball strikes the apex of a three-ball rack and the balls spread across the table.
- [`pot_lid_drop`](pot_lid_drop/README.md): A pot lid slips off a pot on the counter, lands edge-first on the tiled floor, tips over and flops flat.
- [`ramp_car_ball_collision`](ramp_car_ball_collision/README.md): An unpowered toy car rolls down a toy ramp and hits a resting ball on a living-room floor.
- [`soccer_ball_collision`](soccer_ball_collision/README.md): Two soccer balls roll towards each other on grass, collide and slow after rebounding.
- [`soccer_step_collision`](soccer_step_collision/README.md): A soccer ball rolls up a granite kerb step in a school courtyard and strikes a resting basketball.
- [`stair_ball_bounce`](stair_ball_bounce/README.md): A 14 cm hollow toy ball bounces down five wooden stairs onto the landing and rolls on.
- [`tennis_bottle_collision`](tennis_bottle_collision/README.md): A rolling tennis ball hits between two upright empty plastic bottles in a living room.
- [`toy_car_bottle`](toy_car_bottle/README.md): A toy car strikes a plastic water bottle, which topples, slides and comes to rest.
- [`toy_car_collision`](toy_car_collision/README.md): A moving toy car rear-ends a stationary toy car in a furnished room.
- [`toy_car_track_jump`](toy_car_track_jump/README.md): A red toy car climbs an orange plastic track, launches off its lip, lands on the floor and rolls to a stop.
- [`twin_pendulum_collision`](twin_pendulum_collision/README.md): The left of two slightly separated pendulums is released and strikes the other; the motion transfers and decays.
- [`twin_ramp_collision`](twin_ramp_collision/README.md): Red and blue balls released together on a symmetric double ramp collide on the flat middle and roll back up.
- [`u_track_ball`](u_track_ball/README.md): A ball runs down the long branch of a U-shaped track, across the short branch and into a cardboard box.
- [`uneven_ground_ball`](uneven_ground_ball/README.md): A toy ball rolls along an uneven dirt trail in a pine forest, deflecting and slowing on the irregular surface.
- [`volleyball_corner_rebound`](volleyball_corner_rebound/README.md): A volleyball thrown into a room corner bounces off the floor and both walls and rolls out with spin.
- [`wall_clock_drop`](wall_clock_drop/README.md): A wall clock falls off its hook onto a pine crate, tips the crate over and slides to the floor.
