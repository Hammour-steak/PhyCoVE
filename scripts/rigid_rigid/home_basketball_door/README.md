# home_basketball_door

A basketball is thrown across a bedroom at a half-open door, knocks the leaf almost shut, rebounds off it and rolls across the floor until it stops; hinge friction stops the leaf. PyBullet solves the ball and the hinged leaf, and Blender renders the result. This is a rigid–rigid scene of PhyCoVE, the benchmark for physical counterfactual video editing (PCVE). The source video is 4 s at 24 fps, 96 frames, 1280×720.

## Scene

- **Inputs:** `assets/scenes/home_basketball_door/scenario.json` holds the physics, the room layout (`layout.room`: windows and furniture) and the camera; `scene_config.py` loads, merges and validates it. `assets/scenes/home_basketball_door/door_leaf.json` stores the measured door leaf (size, hinge, closing direction); the renderer copies it next to each case and the simulator reads it there.
- **Room:** the same bedroom as `door_basketball_collision`, seen from the other end, built by `appearance/room_shell.py` on the simulator's own planes. `layout.room_bounds` gives the west, east and north walls and the stretch of south wall left of the door; `assets/scenes/home_basketball_door/door_leaf.json` gives the plane, opening and ends of the slanted door wall. On the straight walls the front face of the skirting board is the collision plane and the plaster is set back by one board thickness; on the door wall the plaster face is the collision plane and the skirting stands 35 mm proud, which is the simulator's skirt box. A corridor behind the door is generated by `build_hallway`.
- **Door:** a 12 kg leaf, 2.0 m tall and 45 mm thick, in a 0.9 m deep jamb, hinged with limits at 0° (closed) and 60° (open), starting 45° open. The leaf and its fitted frame are imported from `old_door.glb` by the shared `scripts/pcve_door_asset.py` (shared with door_basketball_collision).
- **Ball:** a 0.62 kg basketball of radius 0.121 m, thrown from (−1.3567, 2.3203, 1.4) m at 7.2 m/s towards a contact point at 72 % of the leaf width and 1.02 m height, with a first-order gravity lead so the throw arrives where it is aimed. The ball is thrown from inside the room, so it can only push the leaf towards closed.
- **Furniture:** a chest of drawers with books under the window, a cube shelf on the west wall (a vase and books on top, a succulent in one cube), a potted plant in the south-east corner, and a bed in the north-west corner (mostly out of frame). The simulator does not know about the furniture, so the renderer checks in every frame the distance between the ball and every furniture bounding box (`room_shell.check_clearance`) and fails below 2 cm; the closest across the cases is the shelf, 0.22 m.
- **Lighting:** a window in the south wall left of the door (in the picture; a garden panorama outside, visible to camera rays only) and a window in the east wall behind the camera for fill. A sun lamp and a physical sky enter only through the windows; there are no fill cards, and the corridor lamp is 3 W. Both faces of the window glass use the air-to-glass Fresnel reflection, so the window seen at about 49° does not turn black from total internal reflection.
- **Rendering:** Cycles, 96 samples by default, a 0.25-frame shutter (`layout.motion_blur_shutter`) because the ball is thrown, and the colour grade from `layout.grade` (default AgX with the Punchy look, exposure −0.2).

## Physics

- **Solver:** PyBullet, 60 substeps per frame (dt = 1/1440 s), 150 solver iterations, gravity −9.81 m/s². Video frame i is the solver state at t = (i−1)/24 s.
- **Door:** a slab on a vertical revolute joint, so gravity exerts no torque on it. It stops because the hinge takes energy out: a fixed viscous `door_hinge_damping` of 0.02 and a Coulomb hinge friction `door_hinge_friction` of 1.0 N·m. The friction is applied before every substep with `applyExternalTorque` as a constant braking torque about the hinge axis, opposite to the leaf's rotation, and not at all while the leaf is at rest (`apply_hinge_friction`). With a constant torque the leaf stops after I·ω²/(2τ) radians and ω goes as 1/I, so the swing scales as 1/I against leaf mass and 1/τ against hinge friction; a viscous term alone would make the swing angle independent of the leaf mass.
- **Ball:** restitution 0.5, friction 0.7, rolling friction 0.03, spinning friction 0.0004, linear damping 0.01, angular damping 0.02.
- **Room:** floor friction 0.6 and restitution 1.0; four static 0.2 m thick walls with friction 0.5 and restitution 0.45, with the door aperture cut through one of them.
- **Timed edits:** a `{frame, params}` schedule written into the running simulation at the top of its frame. The simulator records the hinge state from the live client, switches the friction torque to the new value and continues from the state it has reached; the event log in `physics_transforms.json` records the state on both sides of the write.

## Measured motion

Source: impact on frame 11; the leaf swings from 45.0° to 0.07° (44.93° of travel, peak 1.01 rad/s) and rests against the jamb from frame 33; the ball leaves the impact at 3.92 m/s, traces 6.27 m of floor and ends at (0.91, 3.24) m.

| Case | Outcome | First frame that differs from the source |
|---|---|---|
| `edit_light_ball` | Leaf travel 9.92°, hangs 35.08° open; the lighter ball rebounds faster (4.23 m/s) and runs 6.76 m | 11 |
| `edit_heavy_door` | Travel 10.64°, still 34.36° open at the end, at rest only on frame 77; peak rate 1.01 → 0.11 rad/s; the ball rebounds at 4.33 m/s and runs 6.78 m | 11 |
| `edit_stiff_hinge` | Travel 25.16°, at rest on frame 29 with the leaf 19.84° open; the ball's path matches the source, because the ball has left the leaf two frames after impact | 11 |
| `edit_slow_throw` | Impact moves from frame 11 to frame 20; travel 22.06°, settles 22.94° open on frame 53; the ball rebounds at 3.84 m/s and runs 5.52 m | 2 |
| `edit_stiff_hinge_late` | Travel 23.05°, at rest on frame 26 with the leaf 21.95° open | 13 |

For `edit_stiff_hinge_late` the friction is written at the top of frame 12 (t = 0.4583 s), one frame after the impact, from 1.0 to 5.0 N·m; the leaf angle (41.28°) and rate (−0.994 rad/s) are unchanged across the write. Frames 1–12 are the source, and frame 13 is the first that differs (38.93° against 38.98°). The 4.0 N·m whole-clip edit and the 5.0 N·m edit from frame 12 end 2.1° apart because the swing is already dying by frame 12. The heavy-door and slow-throw edits both take energy out of the impact but differ: the heavy leaf barely yields, so the ball leaves with more speed (4.33 against 3.84 m/s), while the slow throw halves the momentum before contact.

## Cases

| Case | Edit (DSL) | Change |
|---|---|---|
| `source` | — | Ball at 7.2 m/s knocks the 12 kg leaf almost shut |
| `edit_light_ball` | `SET ball.mass TIMES 0.3` | Ball mass 0.62 → 0.186 kg |
| `edit_heavy_door` | `SET door.mass TIMES 10` | Leaf mass 12 → 120 kg |
| `edit_stiff_hinge` | `SET door.friction TIMES 4` | Hinge friction 1.0 → 4.0 N·m |
| `edit_slow_throw` | `SET ball.speed TIMES 0.5` | Throw speed 7.2 → 3.6 m/s |
| `edit_stiff_hinge_late` | `SET door.friction TIMES 5 AT FRAME 12` | From frame 12 (t = 0.458 s) hinge friction 1.0 → 5.0 N·m; frames 1–12 match the source |
| `edit_delete_ball` | `DELETE ball` | Basketball removed from frame 1 |

`edit_vocab.py` defines the objects (`ball`, `door`) and properties: `ball.speed` is an initial condition, so the DSL rejects `AT FRAME` on it, and `door.friction` binds to the hinge friction torque `door_hinge_friction`. The room, jamb and floor are not editable. The builder derives each edit's prompts with `pcve_edit_dsl.make_prompts` and writes each case's `edit_summary`; the source prompts are `DESCRIPTION` in `edit_vocab.py`. The builder names the source case `home_basketball_door_baseline`; the edit IDs are the same as in the published suite (`--list-cases` prints them).

## Requirements

- A Python with `pybullet==3.2.7` (`requirements.txt`), for example `.venv-domino/`; the renderer runs the solve with `--physics-python` (default `$PCVE_PHYSICS_PYTHON`, else `.runtime/sim`, a link to `.venv-domino`).
- Blender 4.3.2: `--blender` (or `$BLENDER_BIN`), or a Python with `bpy` through `--render-python`; otherwise the Blender found by `pcve_assets.blender`.
- `--device auto` (default) prefers an OptiX GPU; `--device cpu` renders on the CPU.

## Commands

Run from the repository root. The builder has no simulate-only mode: the render entry solves each case and records the trajectory next to the render.

```bash
S=scripts/rigid_rigid/home_basketball_door
export PCVE_PHYSICS_PYTHON=.venv-domino/bin/python
B=tools/blender-4.3.2-linux-x64/blender
.venv-domino/bin/python $S/build_pcve_home_basketball_door.py --list-cases
.venv-domino/bin/python $S/build_pcve_home_basketball_door.py --out-root renders/home_basketball_door/new_run --dry-run

# quick check: solve the source and render one tiny preview frame on the CPU
.venv-domino/bin/python $S/build_pcve_home_basketball_door.py --out-root renders/home_basketball_door/check \
  --only home_basketball_door_baseline --mode preview --samples 1 --resolution 64 36 --device cpu \
  --physics-python .venv-domino/bin/python --blender $B

# full 1280x720, 96-sample videos of every case
.venv-domino/bin/python $S/build_pcve_home_basketball_door.py --out-root renders/home_basketball_door/new_run \
  --mode animation --physics-python .venv-domino/bin/python --blender $B

# standalone solve (reads door_leaf.json next to --out, or --door-leaf-json)
.venv-domino/bin/python $S/simulate_home_basketball_door.py --out renders/home_basketball_door/sim/physics_transforms.json \
  --door-leaf-json assets/scenes/home_basketball_door/door_leaf.json
```

- `--mode` is `animation` (default, all 96 frames, writes `video.mp4`) or `preview` (the tail frame only).
- `--only` takes comma-separated case IDs; the source is always built and there is no `--cases` option.
- `--skip-existing` reuses finished cases, `--clean-stale-cases` deletes case folders that are not in the current case list, `--verbose-render` streams the renderer output.
- `tools/render_queue.py` (workflow `batch_render`) samples parameters around the baseline with the shared `pcve_batch_queue.batch_main` (`--count`, `--start-index`, `--seed-base`).

## Outputs

Without `--out-root` the builder writes to `renders/home_basketball_door/suite_build/pcve_home_basketball_door_suite/`. Each case folder `<out-root>/cases/<case>/` holds the prompts, scenario inputs and overrides, a copy of the door leaf, `physics_transforms.json` (with the timed-event log and quality block), `ground_truth_transforms.json`, `scenario_metadata.json`, the saved `.blend` and `video.mp4` (or the preview frame); the root `suite_manifest.json` copies each case's event log and quality block, so the measured values can be checked without re-simulating. The published suite is `renders/pcve_suites/pcve_home_basketball_door_suite/`; do not write new runs into it.

## Code layout

- `build_pcve_home_basketball_door.py`: suite builder (cases, render calls, edit records, manifest).
- `simulate_home_basketball_door.py`: PyBullet solve of the thrown ball and the hinged leaf, hinge friction and timed writes.
- `render_home_basketball_door.py`: Blender scene (door, ball, corridor, windows, camera), solve call, replay and checks.
- `scene_config.py`: scenario loader, editable parameter ranges and timed-edit validation.
- `edit_vocab.py`: edit vocabulary, simulator bindings, edit list with measured summaries, and source prompts.
- `appearance/room_shell.py`: bedroom walls, windows, daylight, furniture placement and the furniture clearance check.
- `tools/render_queue.py`: seeded parameter samples (workflow `batch_render`).
- `entrypoints.json`, `scene_manifest.json`, `requirements.txt`: entry-point description, scene manifest, Python dependency.

## Limitations

- The ball is a rigid sphere; inflation and shell deformation are not modelled.
- Hinge friction is a constant Coulomb torque plus a small viscous term; contact parameters are effective solver values, not measurements.
- The furniture has no collision geometry; the clearance check only guarantees the ball does not pass through it.
- The render sample count of the published videos is not recorded in the published suite.
