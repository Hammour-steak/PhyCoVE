# Asset credits

Car Concept by Eric Chadwick / Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH (2024), CC BY 4.0.
Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept
Original source car by Unity Fan was released under CC0. The Khronos distribution is CC BY 4.0.
The source model is normalized to the toy wheelbase, its body proportions adapted to a diecast toy, repainted copper, its sample lamps switched off, and its four wheels independently matched to the frozen solver axles. Round wheel openings were cut; the original bonnet silhouette is preserved, with the complete visual body raised by 7 mm and the nose slightly shortened to meet a thin flush contact trim. Original UVs are preserved; custom split normals are corrected by the exact inverse-transpose affine and local shape Jacobian.

Poly Haven potted_plant_02 and grass_medium_01 are CC0. The courtyard paving, boundary, window hinges and other added construction are project-authored geometry.

Walls and ceiling use the scanned Poly Haven `beige_wall_001` plaster maps (CC0, https://polyhaven.com/a/beige_wall_001); the sofa, its two pillows and the curtains use the Poly Haven `rough_linen` scan (CC0). The BlenderKit rally car and the Khronos ToyCar used in another scene are not used.
