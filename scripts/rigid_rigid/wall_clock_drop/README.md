# wall_clock_drop

A wall clock comes off its hook high on the wall of a farmhouse boot room and lands on a pine crate
standing in the corner below: the dial slaps down on the crate's top, pushes the crate over forwards,
and once the crate has tipped through 90° the clock slides off onto the floor and comes to rest face
up while the crate ends up on its side half a metre away. Rigid–rigid scene of PhyCoVE. Source
video: 4 s, 24 fps, 96 frames, 1280×720.

## Scene

- Clock: `wall_clock.glb` scaled to 0.44 m across and 42 mm thick, 1.35 kg. In the solver it is a
  cylinder of radius 0.22 m and height 42 mm. It hangs at (−0.60, −2.556, 1.53) with the dial facing
  into the room. The renderer gives it a cream lacquered dial and a walnut case (`walnut_veneer_02`),
  turns the mesh 180° about its axis so 12 is at the top, sets the hands to 1:52:20 and lifts the
  numerals 0.5 mm off the dial.
- Crate: `wooden_crate.glb`, generated by `tools/make_crate_asset.py` (33 boards of 16 mm pine with
  2 mm bevels), scaled axis by axis to 0.40 × 0.30 × 0.62 m to match the solver box, 1.2 kg, centred
  at (−0.64, −2.30) on the floor. Each board carries its own patch of a pale softwood scan
  (`olive_veneer`).
- Room: built by `appearance/boot_room.py` on the solver's collision planes
  (`assets/scenes/wall_clock_drop/room_colliders.json`): a flagstone floor at z = 0 (top of `floor_sunken`), a pine-boarded hall
  floor at z = 0.05 (`floor_platform`), plaster on the y = −2.579 face of `wall_south` with a
  128.5 mm skirting (`plinth_south`) whose face is at y = −2.500. Boarding painted farmhouse green,
  a window, a back door, a peg rail with a lantern, a boot bench with boots and a hat, a bucket, a
  broom and a stool are decoration from Poly Haven scans.
- Light: daylight only. A sun lamp (strength 4.2, 0.53° disc, elevation 41.8°, heading 35°) through
  the south window lays its patch away from the moving bodies; a physical sky fills three shaded
  windows through sky portals. The south window shows `alps_field_2k.hdr` to camera rays only. AgX
  Base Contrast, exposure 2.2.
- Camera: location (−3.20, 0.20, 2.05) looking at (−0.62, −1.88, 0.70), 30 mm lens, f/6.3 focused
  on the target. Over every case the bodies stay inside the frame (largest normalised screen
  coordinates x 0.39, y 0.95).
- Render: Cycles, 256 samples for the published suite, OpenImageDenoise, a quarter-frame shutter,
  persistent data off.

## Physics

- Solver: PyBullet, 1440 Hz, 150 solver iterations, collision margin 0.5 mm.
- Initial state: the clock leaves the hook with a 0.60 rad/s tip-over angular velocity (about x,
  outwards about its top edge) and 0.42 m/s away from the wall.
- Clock friction 0.42, rolling friction 0.003, restitution 0.22. Crate friction 0.55, rolling
  friction 0.004, spinning friction 0.006, restitution 0.15. Floor friction 0.52, floor restitution
  0.16, walls and furniture restitution 0.18. Gravity −9.81 m/s².
- Static colliders: two floor levels, four walls, the south skirting, and a stack of boxes, a desk,
  a chair and a pile of books the crate could slide into, all from `assets/scenes/wall_clock_drop/room_colliders.json`.
  `physics/room_colliders.py` checks that the file belongs to this scene and that every box is well
  formed. The panelling and the skirting are separate colliders.

## Measured motion

1. 0–0.38 s: the clock tips outwards while falling and is carried about 15 cm from the wall.
2. 0.377 s: the clock rim lands on the rear edge of the crate's top (`first_crate_impact_sec`); it
   never touches the wall (`first_wall_impact_sec = null`).
3. 0.38–0.79 s: the dial slaps flat on the crate's top (centre of mass 0.844 → 0.641 m).
4. 0.79–1.96 s: the crate topples forwards about its front bottom edge to 90°
   (`crate_tilt_max_deg`).
5. 1.76 s: the clock slides off onto the floor (`first_floor_impact_sec`) and turns face up.
6. The crate slides on its side to 0.47 m (`crate_displacement_m`). After 2.6 s both bodies are at
   rest (final speed 0.08 mm/s, largest penetration 2.6 mm); the clock rests at
   (−0.60, −1.24, 0.05), 1.04 m from the skirting.

## Validation

- `quality.valid` requires a largest penetration below 12 mm, no body leaving the room and the last
  frame at rest. For the source case the builder also requires that the clock hits the crate, that
  the crate is set in motion (`transmission_observed`) and that it tips at least 45°.
- The renderer refuses a take in which a moving body comes within 2 cm of a decorative piece
  (`check_clearance`) or a decorative piece hides a moving body from the camera (`check_occlusion`).
- All six cases are valid and at rest in the last frame.

## Cases

The builder's native source case is `wall_clock_drop_baseline`; it is published as `source`.

| Case | Edit (DSL) | Change |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `source` | — | Clock hits the crate at 0.377 s; the crate tips 90° and slides 0.47 m |
| `edit_heavy_clock` | `SET clock.mass TIMES 10` | Clock mass 1.35 → 13.5 kg; it reaches the floor at 1.33 s and the crate slides 0.36 m |
| `edit_strong_gravity` | `SET world.gravity TIMES 3` | Gravity −9.81 → −29.43 m/s²; the clock hits the crate at 0.237 s and the crate slides 0.55 m |
| `edit_remove_crate` | `DELETE crate` | Crate absent from frame 1; the clock falls straight to the floor (0.522 s) |
| `edit_bouncier_room` | `SET world.restitution TIMES 4` | Every restitution ×4 (clock 0.88, crate 0.60, floor 0.64, walls and furniture 0.72); the clock bounces 17 cm off the crate and reaches the floor at 1.11 s, the crate tilts 0.2° |
| `edit_gravity_off_after_impact` | `SET world.gravity TIMES 0 AT FRAME 24` | Gravity 0 from frame 24 (0.958 s); the crate stops at 73° and the clock hangs 0.544 m above the floor |
| `edit_remove_clock` | `DELETE clock` | Wall clock removed from frame 1 |

## Assets

- `models` (manual): `wall_clock.glb` (the clock) and `wooden_crate.glb` (the crate, generated by
  `tools/make_crate_asset.py`).
- `polyhaven/wall_clock_drop` (manual): Poly Haven scans for the room surfaces, the decoration and
  the `alps_field_2k.hdr` window view.
- `scenes/wall_clock_drop` (derived): `assets/scenes/wall_clock_drop/room_colliders.json`, the static collider boxes written by
  `tools/export_room_colliders.py`.

Download and check them with `python3 scripts/download_assets.py --scene wall_clock_drop`
(add `--check` to only report).

## Requirements

- Simulation: Python with `pybullet>=3.2.5` (`requirements.txt`; the repository's `.venv-domino`
  has it). CPU only.
- Rendering: Blender 4.3 (`tools/blender-4.3.2-linux-x64`); Cycles on an NVIDIA GPU (CUDA or OptiX)
  for full-quality frames, CPU works for previews.

## Commands

Run from the repository root.

```bash
# Check the registered assets
python3 scripts/download_assets.py --scene wall_clock_drop --check

# List the native case IDs
python3 scripts/rigid_rigid/wall_clock_drop/build_pcve_wall_clock_drop.py --list-cases

# Simulate all six cases on the CPU
PCVE_PHYSICS_PYTHON=.venv-domino/bin/python .venv-domino/bin/python \
    scripts/rigid_rigid/wall_clock_drop/build_pcve_wall_clock_drop.py \
    --out-root renders/wall_clock_drop/suite_build --simulate-only

# Render the simulated cases at the published settings
PCVE_PHYSICS_PYTHON=.venv-domino/bin/python .venv-domino/bin/python \
    scripts/rigid_rigid/wall_clock_drop/build_pcve_wall_clock_drop.py \
    --out-root renders/wall_clock_drop/suite_build --render-only \
    --blender tools/blender-4.3.2-linux-x64/blender --mode animation --resolution 1280 720 --samples 256
```

`tools/render_queue.py` (workflow `batch_render`) samples clock mass, tip-over spin, push, clock
friction and crate mass reproducibly from `--seed-base` and builds each sample with the builder's
options.

## Outputs

The commands above write to `renders/wall_clock_drop/suite_build/`, one directory per case under
`cases/`. Without `--out-root` the builder writes to
`renders/wall_clock_drop/suite_build/pcve_wall_clock_drop_suite/`. The published suite is
`renders/pcve_suites/pcve_wall_clock_drop_suite/`; do not pass it as `--out-root`.

## Code layout

- `build_pcve_wall_clock_drop.py`: suite builder; runs the simulation and render for each case.
- `simulate_wall_clock_drop.py`: PyBullet solve; writes the trajectory and the event log.
- `render_wall_clock_drop.py`: Blender replay and render; writes the video, ground truth and checks.
- `scene_config.py`: physics, render and camera defaults, command-line overrides and validation.
- `edit_vocab.py`: edit DSL vocabulary and case list.
- `physics/scene_geometry.py`: clock, crate, friction and restitution constants and solver settings.
- `physics/room_colliders.py`: loads and checks `assets/scenes/wall_clock_drop/room_colliders.json`.
- `physics/validate_wall_clock_drop.py`: cache validation for `--skip-existing`.
- `physics/input_hashes.py`: asset hashes used by the `--skip-existing` fingerprint.
- `appearance/boot_room.py`: room, decoration, daylight and the clearance and occlusion checks.
- `appearance/look_wall_clock_drop.py`: clock and crate materials.
- `tools/make_crate_asset.py`: generates `wooden_crate.glb` in Blender.
- `tools/export_room_colliders.py`: writes `assets/scenes/wall_clock_drop/room_colliders.json`.
- `tools/render_queue.py`: randomised batch builder.

## Limitations

- The clock is a solid cylinder in the solver; the hands, bezel and glass are visual only.
- The static colliders that are never touched in any case (the north, west and east walls, the
  boxes, desk, chair and books) have no rendered counterpart except the walls.
- In `edit_bouncier_room` the elastic contact impulse goes into the floor, so the crate barely moves
  (0.2°, 2 mm).
