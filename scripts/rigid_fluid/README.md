# Rigid–fluid scenes

Scenes whose primary interaction is between a rigid body and a liquid. There are 16;
`../scene_registry.json` is authoritative and each scene's README gives its physics, cases,
requirements and the commands that rebuild it.

- [`apple_drop_kitchen_basin`](apple_drop_kitchen_basin/README.md): A red apple drops into a basin of water on a kitchen counter, touches the floor and bobs back up (Genesis DFSPH, two-way coupled).
- [`bowling_ball_pool_drop`](bowling_ball_pool_drop/README.md): A 15 cm miniature bowling ball falls into a mountain pool at sunset and sinks to the floor (Genesis DFSPH, two-way coupled).
- [`coffee_ice_drop`](coffee_ice_drop/README.md): An ice cube drops into a mug of coffee, sinks and floats back up to the surface (Genesis DFSPH, two-way coupled).
- [`cooking_oil_pan`](cooking_oil_pan/README.md): A tilted bottle pours cooking oil into a nonstick frying pan on an induction hob (Genesis DFSPH).
- [`cup_water_drop`](cup_water_drop/README.md): A mug of water drops onto a table and lands upright; the water erupts into a crown and settles (Genesis DFSPH, two-way coupled).
- [`faucet_sink`](faucet_sink/README.md): A faucet runs into a closed sink basin for four seconds and the water collects (Mantaflow FLIP).
- [`fish_tank_toy`](fish_tank_toy/README.md): A die-cast toy car falls into an aquarium, sinks and settles on the glass bottom (rigid-body model driving Mantaflow FLIP).
- [`honey_spoon_toast`](honey_spoon_toast/README.md): Honey runs off a fixed spoon onto toast, first as a falling drop and then as a continuous thread (Genesis DFSPH with a viscous thread and film model).
- [`juice_dispenser_orange_cup`](juice_dispenser_orange_cup/README.md): A countertop dispenser pours orange juice into a drinking glass (Mantaflow FLIP).
- [`milk_pitcher_glass`](milk_pitcher_glass/README.md): A tipped milk carton pours milk into a clear glass on a kitchen counter (Genesis DFSPH).
- [`tilted_cup_table_pour`](tilted_cup_table_pour/README.md): Water drains over the rim of a tilted ceramic cup and spreads across a wooden worktop (Genesis DFSPH).
- [`toy_boat_pool_glide`](toy_boat_pool_glide/README.md): A toy tug pushed across an inflatable kiddie pool glides to rest, raising a bow wave and wake (Genesis DFSPH, two-way coupled).
- [`toy_car_float_bathtub`](toy_car_float_bathtub/README.md): A toy car drops onto an inflatable float in a bathtub, pushes it under and rides it as it bobs back up (Genesis DFSPH, two-way coupled).
- [`toy_car_ramp_pool_genesis`](toy_car_ramp_pool_genesis/README.md): A toy car rolls down a ramp into an inflatable pool, splashes in and floats (Genesis DFSPH, two-way coupled).
- [`traditional_tea_pour`](traditional_tea_pour/README.md): Tea pours from a teapot spout into a small cup on a tea table (Genesis).
- [`wine_pour`](wine_pour/README.md): A tilted wine bottle pours red wine into a stemless glass at a bar (Mantaflow FLIP).
